# One entry per line:   forms | kind | meaning | today | verses
#   forms   comma-separated, lowercase; a form with spaces is a phrase
#   kind    archaic   a word or form no longer in ordinary use
#           changed   still in use, but the KJV meaning is different (a "false friend")
#           unit      an old measure, weight or coin
#   meaning what it means in the KJV
#   today   (optional) what a modern reader is likely to assume instead
#   verses  (optional) "@ Book 1:2; Book 3:4": the entry applies only in these verses, for words
#           that keep their modern meaning everywhere else
#           "@! Book 1:2; Book 3:4": the entry applies everywhere EXCEPT these verses
# Lines starting with # are comments. The definitions are written for the KJV's own usage.
